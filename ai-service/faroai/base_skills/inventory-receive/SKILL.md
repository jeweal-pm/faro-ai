---
name: inventory-receive
description: Prepare a receipt against a GIS-approved purchase order with confirmation.
---

Use GIS read tools to identify the purchase order and verify its current approval status before preparing a receipt. Never infer approval, quantity received, location or product identifiers. Ask only for required missing receipt details. Show the GIS preview and wait for explicit user confirmation. FaroAI cannot approve a purchase order; GIS must report it approved before receipt preview and the current tenant must separately approve the receive tool.
